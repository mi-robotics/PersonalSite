03 — CVPR 2027 · In preparation

Efficient Distillation of Deep Thought VLAs: Rectified Depth

Milo Carroll · First author

Shows the recurrent-depth transformer action head is a contraction, distilling its 32-step converged latent into a single-step generator that matches full depth (93.1% vs. 90.5% on LIBERO) at 8.7× lower latency — 250 Hz inference.

Milo Carroll

From a single real-world image and a text instruction, a world model (Cosmos) rolls out a video and key-point flow of the task; the planned flow then conditions a policy that carries it out in simulation.

06 — arXiv 2026 · Preprint

SCDP: Learning Humanoid Locomotion from Partial Observations via Mixed-Observation Distillation

Milo Carroll · First author

A diffusion-based humanoid locomotion policy that conditions on onboard sensor histories while supervising against privileged future state-action trajectories, removing the need for explicit state estimation at deployment.

07 — 2024

Learning Versatile Robot Locomotion: Conditional Adversarial Imitation Learning with Disentangled Skill Conditions

Milo Carroll

A single unified quadruped policy, learned by conditional adversarial imitation from unstructured motion data, that performs flips, handstands, galloping and multiple gaits, interpolates between skills, and is reused by a visual task-level policy for robot parkour.
